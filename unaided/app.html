    What this is

    Every session is a fresh set of short tasks you do with no assistant, no search and no calculator: hold a passage in memory, do arithmetic in your head, estimate a quantity, write a paragraph, follow a chain of reasoning, spell, and keep your bearings.

    Your Unaided score is not a measure of intelligence and makes no health claim. It is a record of what you can still do by yourself, and how that changes as you hand more to machines. Pilots hand-fly on purpose so they can when it counts. This is that, for the rest of us.

    Scores stay on this device. Nothing is sent anywhere, except the paragraph you write, which can be graded by Claude if you allow it.
